Accessibility

Stairs, lifts and ramps come from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete. Step-free journeys get at least 7 minutes at every change, wheelchair journeys 10. The timetable doesn’t say which trains and buses have step-free boarding, so check with the operator when it matters.

Walking speed

Normal
Slow
Fast

Used for every walk: to the stop, between vehicles and to the door. It decides whether a change is possible. Walking routes follow paths from © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) and © swisstopo (swissTLM3D).

With “Adjust to how I walk” on, the app measures your pace while it is open. When another step between Slow and Fast fits you better, the speed changes at the next start and the app tells you. Moving the slider yourself starts the measuring again.

Private. Your walking pace is measured and kept only in this app, on this device. It is never sent anywhere, no places are stored, and turning the setting off deletes it.
