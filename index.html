Language

Other languages are translated by Google Translate, so the text on the screen is sent to Google. Stop, line and place names stay as they are written on the signs.

Accessibility

Stairs, lifts and ramps come from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete. Step-free journeys get at least 7 minutes at every change, wheelchair journeys 10. The timetable doesn’t say which trains and buses have step-free boarding, so check with the operator when it matters.
